## The entry

> simustall — when two people start talking at the same time and both stop to let the other go first.

**Simustall** names the tiny traffic jam that politeness causes: two people begin speaking at the exact same moment, both stop, both wait for the other to go first — and the conversation stalls precisely because everyone involved is being polite. The word blends *simultaneous* and *stall*, so its sound mirrors its meaning: a collision up front, then a dead stop. Three syllables, easy to say, easy to spell, and instantly usable the next time it happens to you ("we just simustalled").
